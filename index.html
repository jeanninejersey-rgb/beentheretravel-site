What you can do

A personal atlas of everywhere life has taken you.

Not just a list of pins. BeenThere keeps the moment, the people and the date, so a place becomes a memory you can revisit.

1

Your travel map

Pin places you have been and the places you dream of going. Keep Been and Bucket List side by side.

2

Memories with a story

Add the date, who you were with, a few words and your photos. Search them any time.

3

Travel Passport

Collect passport stamps and adventure awards as your footprint grows across countries, states and cities.

4

Your Year in Travel

Every year is a chapter. Look back at where life took you.

5

Travel Replay

Watch your journeys play out in order on the map, and create a shareable video.

6

Life Chapters

Group places into stories like Family Vacations, College Years or Our Honeymoon.

How it works

From a trip to a keepsake in three steps.

1

Pin a place

Search for somewhere you have been, or tap the map.

2

Save the memory

Add the date, your story and the photos that make it yours.

3

Watch it grow

Your passport, stamps and yearly recap build themselves as you add places.
